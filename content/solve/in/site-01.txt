Q 13720 [radio] Which question is most appropriate in a formal written enquiry?

 26716) C. To whom should I address my request?
 26717) D. To who should I address my request?
  SITE FEEDBACK: ‣ Options A, B and C are grammatically possible. Option A is neutral, while option B uses the more formal whom but leaves the preposition at the end. We do not normally use who immediately after a fronted preposition, as in option D.

Q 13708 [radio] The lack of affordable housing has ____ the increase in homelessness in many cities.
 26670) A. contributed to
 26671) B. stemmed from
 26672) C. arisen from
 26673) D. been attributed to
  SITE FEEDBACK: ‣ Contributed to means 'helped cause something'. It is used when something is one of several factors behind a result. Stemmed from and arisen from mean 'been caused by something', so they would reverse the relationship. Been attributed to means 'been believed to be caused by something'. It is normally used with the result, for example The increase in homelessness has been attributed to a lack of affordable housing, not with the cause itself.
Q 13709 [radio] The new policy ____ major improvements in the way the company operates.
 26674) A. stemmed from
 26675) B. brought about
 26676) C. arose from
 26677) D. was attributed to
  SITE FEEDBACK: ‣ Bring about means ‘cause something to happen’, especially a change, improvement, or development. Stem from, arise from, and be attributed to all present the subject as the result, so they would suggest that the improvements caused the policy, reversing the relationship.
Q 13710 [radio] The sudden power outage ____ a wave of complaints from customers.

 26681) D. was attributed to
